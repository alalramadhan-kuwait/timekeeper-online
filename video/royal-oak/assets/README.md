# Real photographs of the Royal Oak (not in git)

The film shows the watch only as real photographs of a reference 5402ST. Drop these files here (jpg, png or webp);
until then the render shows a REAL PHOTO NEEDED panel in their place and `render.mjs --final` refuses to run.

| id | what | used in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ro_front` | Straight-on front view with the whole bracelet, cut out on a transparent background (PNG). Set `RO_ASPECT` (width / height) when it differs from 0.42. | the scale, the Basel stand, the final shot |
| `ro_finish` | Macro of brushed surfaces and polished bevels (case or bracelet), portrait or square | "finished like a precious watch" |
| `ro_bezel` | Macro of the octagonal bezel and its hexagonal screws | "reference 5402" |

Use photographs you own or have permission to use, and note the source and licence beside each file.
