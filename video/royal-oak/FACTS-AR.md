# Facts in the two-part Arabic film

Main source: Audemars Piguet's own archive article, AP Chronicles, "Birth of an Icon"
(https://apchronicles.audemarspiguet.com/en/article/birth-of-an-icon), plus the sources in FACTS.md.

| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 / 1 | 1954, Genta aged 23, designs the Polerouter for Universal Genève, for SAS's flights over the North Pole | Universal Genève magazine ("at just 23 years of age"; he was born 1 May 1931, so 22 before May); Wikipedia; SAS 1954 advertisement for the Copenhagen–Los Angeles polar route (Wikimedia Commons, public domain) |
| 1 / 2 | It launched as the "Polarouter", renamed Polerouter in 1957; the photo shows a later "Polerouter" dial, so its label carries no year | Universal Genève magazine |
| 1 / 3 | Omega asked him to refresh the Constellation (AP dates the refresh to 1959) | AP Chronicles; Sotheby's, "Gérald Genta: the most visionary watch designer of the 20th century". | 1 / 4 | His name appears in AP's archives in 1960; Model 5179 (circle, square, rectangle, 1961), asymmetric 5182 (1962). AP says both were "probably" designed by him | AP Chronicles. The 1969 Le Brassus building photo is no longer shown under 1960 |
| 1 / 5 | 1967: honourable mention at the Prix de la Ville de Genève for an AP handbag watch, Model 8311. The rosette reads تنويه (mention), not a win | AP Chronicles; AP catalogue page (`m8311_sketch.jpg` is that page; `m8311.jpg` is the design drawing, c. 1966) |
| 1 / 6 | Georges Golay, at AP since 1945, managing director from 1967, the first not from the founding families. The 1966 photo is labelled with its year only | AP Chronicles |
| 1 / 7 | "We were like a tandem… if one of us didn't agree, we didn't make the model"; both wore Model 5233 | AP Chronicles (Genta's words) |
| 1 / 8 | April 1970: Genta was 38 (born 1 May 1931), 16 years after the Polerouter; on the 10th at 4 pm the phone rang | AP Chronicles; Wikipedia |
| 2 / 0 | 10 April 1970, before the Basel fair: Golay met Carlo de Marchi (Turin), Charles Bauty (Lausanne), Charles Dorot (Paris) | AP Chronicles |
| 2 / 1 | Gold alone was not enough; a steel watch, sporty and elegant, for the way people lived; AP calls them the Three Musketeers | AP Chronicles |
| 2 / 2 | Golay's words: a steel sports watch never done before, sketch by tomorrow morning | AP Chronicles (Genta's account) |
| 2 / 5 | The original drawing, night of 10–11 April 1970, gouache and pencil, signed; shown upright (`genta_gouache.jpg`; `sketch_original.jpg` is the same drawing turned 90°). Not the c. 1972 watercolour AP bought in 2022 | AP Chronicles; © Gérald Genta Heritage Association |
| 2 / 6 | CHF 3,300 in 1972 (other sources: 3,650 at launch, 3,750 in 1975); "the costliest steel watch in the world" is the 1972 advertising slogan, credited as such on screen. Calibre 2121 shown in AP's 1971 archive photo | AP Chronicles |
| 2 / 7 | Basel, 15 April 1972, reference 5402ST, 39 mm across the case (not the crown), about 7 mm thick (case sheet: 38.7 × 48 × 7.3 mm). Genta is not shown at AP's stand: he left AP that year and presented his own brand at the same fair | AP Chronicles (5402 page, case sheet `ro5402_fiche.jpg`, first trade publication *The Swiss Watch* 2/1972) |
