# Reel: «اسأل محمد»

A short, funny vertical reel introducing the owners' new stock assistant in the
Time Keeper back office, narrated in the Ali Alyousifi voice (TK Voice, V5).

## The feature, in plain words

- A small round face sits in the corner of every page of the back office:
  Mohammed, a drawn character with round glasses and a navy blazer.
- Tap him and a chat opens. Ask about stock, sales, brands, or what to buy,
  in Kuwaiti Arabic or English.
- He answers from Lightspeed's own figures. Every number in his answer is
  checked against the data before it is shown; if he cannot back a number,
  he does not say it.
- He gives buy advice with reasons (how fast it sells, stock left, what's on
  order), keeps follow-up questions in context ("and at the Avenues only?"),
  and for consignment stock says "don't buy, ask the supplier".
- Only the three owners see him.

## Format

- 1080 × 1920, 30–45 s, Kuwaiti Arabic narration with burned-in Arabic captions.
- Tone: light and funny, a wink rather than slapstick. Mohammed is the calm,
  always-right colleague; the "before" world is chaos.
- Visuals: the paper-story style, with `mohammed-cartoon.png` (transparent) as
  the character and the phone screenshots in `screens/` as the product shots.
- End card: Time Keeper.
- Credits line (required by `video/brand/VOICE-CONSENT.md`):
  «الراوي: صوت ذكاء اصطناعي من صوت علي اليوسفي، بموافقته».

## Data rule

Screens and narration use illustrative figures only. No real costs, margins,
stock values or sales totals. The screenshots here were taken with the margin
and stock value removed; keep it that way.
