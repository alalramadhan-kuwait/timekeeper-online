## What I need from you to pass each gate

1. **Timeline:** the years and opening dates marked "?", what 2018 marks, and the order of the Lounge, Time Gallery and The Avenues. Open the PUBLIC items and tell me if any is wrong.
2. **Brand:** the official logo, the font files, and either access to the website and Instagram or screenshots.
3. **Assets:** the Required list, starting with labelled founder photos.
4. **Characters:** labelled photos, each founder's consent, and your approval of the head-from-real-photo method.
5. **Narration:** the voice rule (they until 2018, we after, they again at the close), the hook you prefer for each episode, and your edits.
