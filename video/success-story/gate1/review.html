2 · Brand system

The identity is monochrome: black ink on warm paper. So the film has no accent colour. Emphasis comes from black and white inversion and from the real photographs.

Type

  • Arabic headlines, brand templateBahij Helvetica Neue Bold. Licensed and not available here, so the draft uses Cairo ExtraBold until the files arrive.
  • EnglishUnknown. The draft uses Inter Tight. Needs the website or guideline.

Logo and motifs

  • LogoBlack script "tk" inside a faint dial ring with four tick marks. Shown as a paper tile at the end of Episode 1, the end of Episode 3 and on the covers. Never first.
  • Motifs from the logoThe ring and ticks become a minute-track timeline with the year in a date window, and a hand-style radial wipe. No gears.
  • PaperWarm neutral paper for light scenes, graphite and ink for night and museum scenes. Captions are black on light and white on dark, the same inversion as the brand’s carousel boxes.

Could not inspect

4 · Founder characters

Nothing is generated and no credits are spent. No labelled founder photos have arrived, and I do not match faces to names.

  • Six characters, not threeEach founder in two periods, student years and today, because the ending cuts back to the young friends.
  • MethodThe head is cut from the founder’s own photograph, so likeness is guaranteed. Body, arms and legs are generated paper-craft pieces. If it looks wrong, the whole figure is generated from the photo and checked. One founder is tested first.
  • ToolsImage generation is connected with 141 credits. Nano Banana Pro takes reference images. One trial is quoted before more are run.
