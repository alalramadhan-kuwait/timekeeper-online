## Colour

The identity is monochrome. The brand template uses only black and white boxes and the logo is black ink on warm off-white. So the film has **no accent colour**. Emphasis comes from black and white inversion and from the real photographs, which carry all the colour.

## Logo

- The mark is a black script "tk" with a long vertical stroke and a crossbar, inside a faint dial ring with four tick marks (12, 3, 6, 9).
- In the film it sits on a paper tile with a thin white margin, as a paper-cut piece with a physical shadow. Never recoloured, never on a busy photograph.
- It appears only at three moments: the end of Episode 1, the end of Episode 3, and the covers. It is **not** the first image of any episode.
- The official vector file is needed. The draft uses the app icon.

## Graphic motifs, taken from the logo itself

- The **dial ring with four ticks** becomes the timeline: a minute track along the bottom of milestone scenes, with the year in a date window (2018, 2019, 2022).
- A **radial wipe** that sweeps like a watch hand for chapter changes. A circular bezel wipe for photo reveals.
- No gears. The brief warns against the cliché and the logo does not use them.

## Paper system

- Warm neutral paper for light scenes, graphite and ink for night, museum and international scenes. Colour comes only from photographs, so a real photo on paper is always the most colourful thing in frame.
- **Banners:** off-white torn strip, black text, muted grey tape. Milestones only.
- **Caption tags:** black tag with white text on light paper, white tag with black text on dark paper (the same inversion as the brand's carousel boxes). Three to four words at a time.
- **Photographs:** polaroid, taped photo, postcard, contact sheet, passport page. Never cropped into a face.
- **Texture:** fibre paper, hand-cut edges, soft shadows, stop-motion jitter at 12 frames a second.
- **Personality:** a premium watch magazine rebuilt from paper. Restrained. Negative space is part of the design.

## Physical brand cues from the supplied store photograph

Pale warm stone and marble, dark glass, soft overhead light, lettering in muted brushed metal: **TIME GALLERY** in widely spaced capitals with the Arabic name beneath. The film's paper tones and the stone of the store should read as one family. Please confirm this reads as the real store look.
