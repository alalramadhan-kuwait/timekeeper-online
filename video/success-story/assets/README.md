Real evidence goes here and is deliberately not committed (photographs of people, character art, narration).

- Photos and screenshots: `<asset id>.jpg|png|webp`, for example `ep1_boulder_university.jpg`. The ids are listed in ../ASSET-REQUEST.md.
- Characters: `characters/<id>/front/<part>.png` and `characters/<id>/side/<part>.png`. See ../CHARACTERS.md.
- Narration per scene: put WAVs named after the scene id next to the storyboards and reference them with `"voice"` in build.py.

Share these files through Time Keeper's own storage, not through git.
